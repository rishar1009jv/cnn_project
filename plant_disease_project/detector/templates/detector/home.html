Key Features

Built for research, designed for reliability

🛡️

Honest AI

No hardcoded results or fake confidence. Predictions come from an actual trained model. Limitations are clearly stated.

🖼️

Image Quality Check

Laplacian-variance blur detection flags poor-quality images before inference, with a clear explanation of the estimate's limitations.

✨

Conservative Enhancement

CLAHE contrast enhancement and gentle denoising applied only when needed. Original image always preserved.

📊

Research Evaluation

Includes accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score evaluation. Blur vs. enhanced comparison analysis included.

📚

Expert Recommendations

Disease management guidance sourced from agricultural extension resources. Chemical instructions are not invented.

🔒

Secure & Validated

All uploads validated server-side. Files are checked for type, size and image integrity before processing.
